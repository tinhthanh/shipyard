---
title: The floating bar
description: Home's most urgent parts over every other app on macOS, full screen ones too, with each login's plan limits and the agents that need you or are working.
---

Home's most urgent parts can also stay in view over every other app, full
screen ones too: turn on **Settings → Appearance → Floating bar** (macOS).
It shows:

- a ring for each Claude Code and Codex login, the outer arc its shortest
  window (Claude Code's session) and the inner one its most used other
  window (a week), amber from 70% and red from 90%. This needs the
  [Usage & accounts](/guides/plugins) plugin on, and is read from each box
  every five minutes;
- how many agents need you, with an amber dot, and how many are working.

On the right edge it waits as a sliver against the screen's edge, amber
when an agent needs you and blue while one works; at the notch it hides in
the notch itself. Hover it to open the bar, whatever app is in front, and
rest on that for a card with each window's reset and the agents'
names; click one to open it in Shipyard. **Where** puts the bar on the
right edge of the screen, or around the notch at the top (the middle of the menu bar
on a screen without one). It closes with Shipyard's window. The design is
after [Codenotch](https://github.com/vinzdg/codenotch) (MIT).
